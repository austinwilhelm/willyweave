a battery swollen  
beaten, and bruised  
how have i come this far  
having felt quite used  
  
a task and chores  
to keep myself busy  
an exhausted feeling  
keeps me in a tizzy  

another day in the books  
water is all but gone  
i'll find a place to recharge  
to bring myself along  


-aw
