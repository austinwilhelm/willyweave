routine and structure  
my old and dearest friend  
our time has come slowly  
but for now this must end  

a walk in my shoes  
and a run to my car  
for you leave me more tied down  
so i cannot go very far  

amid this very lull  
my energy drained low  
i need to escape  
and i know *just* where to go  


-aw
