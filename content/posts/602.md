pairs and pairs and pairs  
glee and joy swirling  
summer rays with colors popping  
the dreaded heat, but a cool breeze  

grass nestled between worming toes  
i've found my roots here  
for this is where i'm meant to be  
a time of grounding and solace  

yet temperatures rise and fall  
and the birds they call  
for lovers and friends alike  
all the while, i'm there - grounded
