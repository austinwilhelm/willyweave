a string so elegantly tied  
from your corner to mine  
there’s no other way it could be  
   
that string i’d once tapped  
and the space between us i’d gapped  
will all have been worthy of what we’d become  
  
a shimmer and a shine  
with a look in her eyes  
told me all i needed to know  
  
for i knew it quite well  
from that voiceless tell  
that you’d be mine forever
