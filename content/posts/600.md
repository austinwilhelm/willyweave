are we significant?  
who we say we are?  
or are we just fleeting  
made of stars from the afar  

if life has meaning  
why is it hard to understand  
for i walk this earth each day  
it surely cannot be planned  

so much to do  
and so much to see  
forget what you thought  
the real fight is to be free  


-aw
