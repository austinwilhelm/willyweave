what does it mean  
to be truly alone  
thoughts that are scary  
yet i feel at home  
  
sometimes, yes  
it's not so fun  
but it's me i look after  
so why would i run?

these thoughts are the fools  
not a part of me  
so i throw out the bad ones  
and i go on being me  


-aw
