hi friend  
i'm back again  
from a place  
not so far away  
  
i was lost here  
a time or two  
with only myself  
to escape to  

where i go   
even the crows know  
it's a place  
of fire and fury   
  
there and back  
and back again  
a magic and worry  
i'll always return in a hurry  
 


-aw
