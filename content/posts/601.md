i'm sorry my friend  
for not being there  
i just want you to know  
that always, i'll care  

a gentle smile  
the awkward goodbyes  
i'll be there through the lows  
to balance out the highs  
  
we've come this far  
and we've plenty more to go  
oh so much to do and see  
where we'll go, i've yet to know  


-aw
