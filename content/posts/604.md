surely i don't need it
i'm doing just fine  
let's chat soon  
or see you another time  

i'm doing alright  
just getting on by  
there's much more to life  
yet i grin and sigh  

a life unbeknownst to me  
but feeling a little stuck  
maybe things will change though    
with my will or with my luck  


-aw
